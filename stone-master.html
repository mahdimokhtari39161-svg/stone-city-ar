STONEMASTER

From Quarry to Masterpiece

A free natural-stone puzzle game

Stone Master is a free puzzle and building game you can play in any browser, on Android, iPhone, tablet or desktop. Start as a beginner stone miner, swap neighbouring stones to match three or more, fill customer orders and grow into a master stone designer.

How it works

  • Match & mine – line up identical travertine, marble and granite blocks on a 7×7 board before you run out of moves.
  • Forge special stones – four in a row makes a Chisel, an L or T shape makes Dynamite and five in a row makes a Master Saw.
  • Beat 30 levels – deliver orders, polish raw slabs and smash boulders across the Quarry, the Workshop and the Showroom.
  • Design façades – spend your coins to clad a villa, a mansion and a palace, then compare before and after.
  • Come back daily – a new daily challenge, login rewards and streak bonuses every day.

Eight natural stones

Abbas Abad Travertine, Haji Abad Travertine, Atashkuh Travertine, Chocolate Travertine, White Marble, Cream Marble, Granite and Silver Travertine. Open Discover Real Natural Stones to explore the real materials.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the game can be installed to your home screen and played offline. Your progress is saved on your device.
